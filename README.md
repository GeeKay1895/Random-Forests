# 📌 Project 1: Sales Prediction using Random Forest Classifier

---

## 🎯 Objective

To predict whether sales will be High or Low based on business and market-related factors.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains variables such as:

- Sales
- Price
- Advertising
- Income
- Population
- Age
- Education
- Shelf Location
- Urban
- US

---

## 🔍 Business Problem

A cloth manufacturing company wants to identify the factors that influence high sales performance.

The project helps:
- Understand sales-driving factors
- Improve marketing strategy
- Optimize pricing decisions
- Improve store-level sales performance

---

## 🧠 Approach

### ✔ EDA
Performed analysis on:
- Sales distribution
- Price vs Sales
- Advertising vs Sales
- Shelf location impact

### ✔ Target Conversion
Sales was converted into:
- High Sales
- Low Sales

to perform classification.

### ✔ Random Forest Model
Used:
- RandomForestClassifier
- Ensemble learning approach
- Multiple decision trees

---

## 📈 Model Performance

- Accuracy Achieved: 75%

---

## 📊 Important Features

Top influential features:
1. Price
2. Age
3. Advertising

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Product pricing strongly affects sales performance.
- Advertising investment positively impacts sales.
- Customer demographics influence purchasing trends.

---

## 🚀 Business Impact

The model helps businesses:
- Optimize pricing strategy
- Improve advertising allocation
- Identify high-performing sales conditions
- Support data-driven decision making

# 📌 Project 2: Fraud Risk Prediction using Random Forest Classifier

---

## 🎯 Objective

To classify individuals into:
- Risky
- Good

based on taxable income and demographic factors.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains:
- Taxable Income
- City Population
- Work Experience
- Undergrad Status
- Marital Status
- Urban Status

---

## 🔍 Business Problem

Organizations want to identify potentially risky individuals based on financial and demographic behavior.

The project supports:
- Fraud analysis
- Risk assessment
- Financial monitoring
- Decision support systems

---

## 🧠 Approach

### ✔ EDA
Performed analysis on:
- Risk distribution
- Population vs Risk
- Work experience patterns
- Marital status analysis

### ✔ Target Conversion
Taxable income was converted into:
- Risky → Taxable Income <= 30000
- Good → Taxable Income > 30000

### ✔ Random Forest Model
Used ensemble learning to improve prediction stability and reduce overfitting.

---

## 📈 Model Performance

- Accuracy Achieved: 73.33%

---

## 📊 Important Features

Top influential features:
1. City Population
2. Work Experience
3. Undergrad Status

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Population demographics influence risk behavior.
- Work experience impacts financial stability.
- Educational background contributes to financial risk patterns.

---

## 🚀 Business Impact

The model helps organizations:
- Identify high-risk individuals
- Improve fraud monitoring
- Support financial risk assessment
- Enable data-driven decision making

# 🌲 Why Random Forest?

Random Forest is an ensemble learning algorithm that:
- Combines multiple Decision Trees
- Improves prediction accuracy
- Reduces overfitting
- Produces stable predictions
- Supports feature importance analysis

---

# 🧠 Ensemble Learning Concept

Instead of relying on a single Decision Tree, Random Forest combines predictions from multiple trees using majority voting.

This improves model reliability and generalization capability.
